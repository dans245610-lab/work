Allow camera access, then hold up your hands.

How to

Every hand gets a fireball. Sign an ASL letter and hold it still for about a second: the letter gets typed at the bottom, and some letters change that hand's effect.

  1. Face your palm toward the camera (most letters) with your hand fully in frame.
  2. Good light helps a lot. Keep your hand roughly a forearm's length from the camera.
  3. The ring around the letter above your hand fills up while you hold it.
  4. To type a double letter, relax your hand for a moment between the two.

Heads up

Recognition is a small model trained on still photos, so it's a guess, not a teacher. Similar shapes (M / N / S / T / E, or K / V) get mixed up the most. J and Z are motion letters: make the shape, then draw the letter in the air.
