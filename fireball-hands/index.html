Guide

Every hand holds a fireball. Sign an ASL letter and hold it still for about a second: the ring above your hand fills up, the letter is typed, and that letter's react plays once.

  1. Palm toward the camera for most letters, whole hand in frame, decent light.
  2. Keep your hand still while the ring fills. Moving cancels it.
  3. J and Z are drawn in the air. Make the shape, then draw a big letter, about two hand-widths wide.
  4. To repeat a letter, relax your hand for a moment between the two.
  5. Tap any card below to practise that letter.

Heads up

Recognition is a small model trained on still photos, so treat it as a guess, not a teacher. Look-alike shapes (M / N / S / T / E, K / V, P / Q) get mixed up the most.
