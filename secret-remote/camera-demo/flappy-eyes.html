Tips

Keep your head fairly still and move your eyes; if precision is poor, it's fine to move your head a little too: calibration learns both. Recalibrate if you move your chair or the light changes. Glasses with strong reflections make it harder.

Honest limits

This is webcam eye tracking: expect roughly ±10–15% of the game's height, not pinpoint. That's why the gaps are wide.

Your camera light will be on; a page can't hide it. Video is analysed on this device and never recorded or sent.
