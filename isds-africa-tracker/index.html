      How to read this

      The firms column is built by matching every ICSID case page against each firm's name, so "All ICSID" counts every case where the firm appears as counsel on any proceeding, including annulments. Large firms change names (Herbert Smith Freehills is now HSF Kramer), so counts can be slightly off.

      Leverage notes point to where outside attention lands: listed-company boards and their other directorships, institutional and state shareholders, funders whose return depends on the award, and the firms of record. Everything here is a public business role. No home addresses or personal contact details are collected.
