🎼 Maestro

Learn the beat patterns real conductors use. The camera tracks your conducting hand, finds each beat (the point where your hand changes direction), and grades the direction and timing against the pattern.

The patterns

Each beat is a stroke in one direction, and the beat lands where the stroke ends. Make each stroke clear, about a hand-length or more, and change direction crisply on the beat.
