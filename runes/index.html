🪄 Rune Caster

Draw runes in the air with your finger to cast spells.

  • Point your index finger (other fingers curled) to start drawing. A glowing trail follows your fingertip.
  • Open your hand (or stop pointing) to cast the rune you drew.
  • Ghosts drift down wearing runes. Cast a rune to blast every ghost wearing it. Three ghosts get past and it's over.
  • Draw big, about a hand-length or more, in one smooth stroke.
