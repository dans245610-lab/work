    When to use it

    Welcome to Little Hands 👋

    Before you start, a few things worth knowing:

    • It's private. Your camera picture is analysed right here on your device. Nothing is uploaded, recorded or shared, and the app works offline.
    • It's practice, not a teacher. These are signs from ASL, a full language of the Deaf community. Watch Deaf signers in the linked videos, and take a class from a Deaf instructor if you can.
    • The camera feedback is approximate. It checks hand shape, place and movement with simple rules, so it can be fooled and can miss good signs. Your baby's versions will be much rougher, and that's completely normal.
    • It isn't medical advice. If your baby is deaf or hard of hearing, or you're unsure, talk to your doctor and see the family resources on the resources page.
