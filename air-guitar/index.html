🎸 Air Guitar Hero

Hold chord shapes with one hand and strum with the other. Your camera watches both: hit each chord gem as it crosses the line, with the right shape, on time.

The four chords

Real chord charts on the left of each card, the air shape your hand makes on the right. More fingers up means a different chord; a fist mutes.

How to play

Left hand (left side of the screen) holds the chord shape, palm toward the camera. Right hand strums: swipe it down (or up) through the middle of your strum zone, like brushing strings. Keep both hands in frame, with a little space between them.
